More goddess & boho braids work

Choosing your curls

Goddess and boho braids mix braided sections with loose curls, so the style moves and looks full. You bring or buy the curly braiding hair, so pick the color and type you like best, and message Abla if you would like a second opinion before you buy.

Length changes the look and the time. A bob stays light and swingy, while longer lengths use more hair and take more hours.

What to expect at your appointment

Plan on about four to five hours, and longer for longer lengths or more curls. Bring a charger and something to watch. Abla confirms the final price before she starts. The first visit guide covers how to prepare.

Taking care of the curls

Curly ends can tangle sooner than the braided sections, so a satin bonnet at night helps. Most boho styles last about six to eight weeks. See the aftercare guide for the basics.

Other ways to wear curls

Want curls with a different pattern? Look at Fulani braids, which have a boho version, or the knotless braids that include a boho knotless bob.

Questions

What hair should I buy for boho braids?

Bring curly braiding hair in the color you want. If you are unsure of the exact type, ask Abla before your appointment.

How long do boho braids last?

Most last about six to eight weeks. Curly ends can tangle sooner, so a satin bonnet at night helps.

Can I get a bob length?

Yes. The boho knotless bob is listed on the services page.
